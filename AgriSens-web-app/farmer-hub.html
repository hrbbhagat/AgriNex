Recent Expert Agronomist Answers

Wheat Crop Asked by Ramesh K. (Punjab)

"What is the ideal Urea application timing during tillering stage?"

Expert Answer: Apply top-dressing Urea (25kg/acre) immediately following first irrigation (21 days post sowing) for optimum nitrogen absorption.

Cotton Crop Asked by Suresh P. (Gujarat)

"How to prevent Pink Bollworm infestation naturally?"

Expert Answer: Deploy Pheromone traps @ 5 traps/acre and spray Neem seed kernel extract (NSKE 5%) during early boll formation.
