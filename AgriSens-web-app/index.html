FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions about Agro Space AI tools, soil testing, or Mandi rates? We've got answers.

How accurate are the Agro Space Machine Learning models?

Our models are trained on extensive agricultural datasets containing thousands of soil samples and crop diagnostic records across diverse agro-climatic zones, achieving over 98% accuracy in crop recommendation and disease identification.

Is Agro Space available in regional Indian languages?

Yes! Agro Space includes built-in multi-language translation support for Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, Punjabi, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, and Assamese via the top navigation bar.

How do I test my soil NPK levels?

You can use standard government soil health card readings or rapid digital NPK soil testing kits. Input Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), pH, and rainfall into our Fertilizer AI tool for immediate dosage recommendations.

Are Mandi commodity prices updated daily?

Yes, Mandi market rates are synchronized daily from official APMC agricultural market databases across major hubs in Uttar Pradesh and nationwide.
