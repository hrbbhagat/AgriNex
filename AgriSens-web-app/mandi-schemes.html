Government Farmer Support

National Welfare & Subsidy Schemes

Key government schemes designed to provide financial aid, crop insurance, and soil health testing.

Financial Aid

PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year transferred into farmer bank accounts in 3 equal installments.

Official PM-KISAN Portal ↗
Crop Insurance

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Comprehensive crop insurance covering yield loss due to non-preventable natural risks, hail, drought, and flood.

Official PMFBY Portal ↗
Soil Health

Soil Health Card Scheme

Provides farmers with soil nutrient status cards every 2 years along with customized fertilizer dosages.
